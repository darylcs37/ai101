Exercise rules and assumptions

All assets share one arbitrary interaction radius in exercise units (EU); no real equipment ranges are represented. Positions are fictional and fixed at reset.

Radars are observer-only. Their contact events are geometric proximity checks, not radar detection or track sharing. Other units use identical abstract tag rules, regardless of depicted role or target class.

Opposing groups must stay within the 3D boundary and outside the safe zone for the dwell interval. Each source may attempt once per opposing asset per continuous contact, spends a token and cools down. Seeded draws determine tag points. No damage or destruction occurs.

Actions use stable asset-ID order, not tactical prioritization. Display toggles only hide graphics; they do not disable assets or change simulation outcomes.

No terrain masking, radar equation, interception, weapon dynamics, or operational air-defence model. Ground graphics use an ellipsoid map with no terrain elevation.

Fictional exercise · radar = observer · AA / SHORADS = generic tag actors
EU are arbitrary exercise units, not operational ranges.
