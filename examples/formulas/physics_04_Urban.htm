CMO implementation checklist

  1. Use native terrain, sensors, weather, doctrine, and database behavior first.
  2. Separate detection, identification, continuity, location error, hit, and functional effect.
  3. Record every heuristic input; distinguish it from a physical identity or calibrated model.
  4. Avoid double-counting engine masking, terrain, or weather penalties.
  5. Check units, elevation datums, bias, correlation, and confidence levels.
  6. Run sensitivity checks rather than presenting poorly known inputs as exact.
