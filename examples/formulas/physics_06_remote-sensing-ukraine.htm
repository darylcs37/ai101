Notation and corrections carried into this HTML

Optical depth is written D to avoid confusion with atmospheric transmission τ or T. Angles in displayed equations may use degrees, but JavaScript trigonometry converts them to radians. Planck calculations use metres internally and convert radiance to per-micrometre units.

  • Horizontal sky-view factor normalization is 1/π, so an unobstructed sky gives SVF = 1.
  • For emitter-row view factors, the diffuse radiosity system uses RF, not an unqualified RFᵀ.
  • Anthropogenic heat is an input: Rn + QF = H + LE + G + ΔQS. Storage and ground terms must not be double-counted.
  • The simple volume LiDAR equation is explicitly separated from hard-target rooftop returns.
  • Camera projection produces image coordinates; orthorectification needs the inverse mapping with elevation.

These clarifications correct ambiguities in the prior equation reference rather than reproduce them in the interactive models.
