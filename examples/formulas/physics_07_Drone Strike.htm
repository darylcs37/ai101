Model assumptions

Airframe: generic electric fixed-wing, empty mass 4.5 kg, wing area S = 0.55 m², aspect ratio AR = 9, Oswald efficiency e = 0.80, zero-lift drag CD₀ = 0.035, propulsive efficiency η = 0.55 (propeller × motor × ESC). Atmosphere: ISA sea level, ρ = 1.225 kg/m³. Battery: 200 Wh/kg pack, 90% usable depth of discharge, 10% reserve floor, linear energy drain (no voltage sag, temperature or Peukert effects). Wind: uniform and steady; no gusts, shear or thermals. Intercept: each zone is a circle with constant engagement hazard λ. Survival through each zone is modelled as a Poisson process, P = e−λt, and zones are treated as independent. Cruise is level, with no climb, loiter or terminal-phase energy.
This is an educational physics demonstrator built on public textbook models: the Breguet-style electric range equation, the navigation wind triangle and exponential exposure survival. The airframe and threat numbers are illustrative and do not represent any real system. Do not use this model for real-world mission planning or targeting.
