What this companion changes

Source: Urban Interceptor Drone Physics — v1.0, darylcs, CC BY 4.0, supplied 9-page PDF. This adaptation changes the scenarios to civilian relief and inspection, adds interactive illustrations, and annotates mathematical assumptions. The source's counter-UAS equations are retained only as conceptual references in Chapters 2–3; there is no interception solver, guidance controller, capture model, weapons integration or real-world route planning.

  1. Closing speed: R is a vector but range is r = ‖R‖. Scalar closing speed is Vc = −(R · Ṙ)/r. Do not equate a scalar to the relative-velocity vector.
  2. Collision-triangle sign: for R = rT − rI and Ṙ = vT − vI, constant-course collision requires R + Ṙt = 0. Thus relative velocity points opposite R, not along it. No collision calculation is implemented.
  3. Fresnel coefficient: the general first-zone radius uses approximately 17.32√[d₁d₂/(f(d₁+d₂))] metres with km/GHz units. The PDF's 8.657 coefficient belongs to the midpoint form 8.657√(D/f), where D is total distance, not to that general expression. This lab uses SI units and r₁ = √(λd₁d₂/(d₁+d₂)). Fresnel derivation.
  4. Energy accounting: Ps = ḣ + (V/g)V̇; ḣ = Ps only at constant speed. The interactive lab exposes the acceleration term. Virginia Tech, Chapter 5.
  5. Aircraft regime: wing lift, drag polar and coordinated-level-turn equations describe a simplified fixed-wing aircraft, not rotor hover. Lift coefficient, stall margin, propulsion and structural limits must also be considered; geometric radius alone is not a feasible-turn certificate. A semicircular reversal spans 2R before margins; street-width versus R alone is not a general clearance test. Virginia Tech, Chapter 8.
  6. Battery accounting: 1 Wh = 3,600 J. Here Plevel is useful aerodynamic power DV, and η converts battery energy to useful propulsion energy. If Pavg were already battery-side electrical draw, endurance would be E/Pavg, without multiplying by η again.
  7. Uncertainty and sensing: σvt represents a temporally correlated velocity-error scale, not a universal law for independent white velocity noise. The bias term is a deterministic magnitude. The PDF does not provide a complete probability-of-detection model; no Pd is inferred from SNR. Its two-pixel EO relation is a geometric threshold, not guaranteed detection.
  8. Wind load: ½ρ(V+u′)²SC is total aerodynamic force under the scalar toy assumption; the incremental gust force subtracts ½ρV²SC. Real canyon flow is not a sealed Venturi and requires more than an area ratio.
