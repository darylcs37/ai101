01 · FICTIONAL DNIPRO RESIDENTIAL BLOCK

Facade survey in a crosswind

A civilian inspection team holds a multirotor stationary beside an apartment facade. Increasing crosswind raises drag quadratically, increasing both tilt and thrust demand.

Change the conditions

Fw = ½ρCDAV²
θ = atan(Fw / mg)
T = √[(mg)² + Fw²]
Pideal = T3/2 / √(2ρAdisk,total)
ρ = 1.225 kg/m³; g = 9.81 m/s². Four 0.43 m rotors. Ideal induced power shown only as a static-equivalent loading estimate; actual crosswind power needs an inflow model.

Watch the tilt rather than just the thrust number. A positive static margin does not prove adequate gust recovery, motor thermal capacity or rotor-level control authority. Building-induced turbulence is not represented.

02 · FICTIONAL KYIV MUNICIPAL INSPECTION SITE

Radio margin and delayed braking

An operator supervises an inspection from a designated ground station. A simplified radio budget and a separate stopping-distance model show why reception strength and control responsiveness must be evaluated independently.

Link and motion inputs

FSPL = 32.44 + 20log₁₀(dkm) + 20log₁₀(fMHz)
Pr = Pt + Gt + Gr − losses
M = Pr − sensitivity − reserves
dstop = vτ + v²/(2a)
Illustrative 2.4 GHz link: 20 dBm transmitter, 2 dBi antennas each, 3 dB system loss, −98 dBm sensitivity; 15 dB fade and 5 dB implementation reserves. Braking a = 3 m/s². Values are not radio-use permissions.

Distance consumed before stopping

Additional urban loss is a user-selected allowance, not a ray-tracing prediction. Fresnel clearance does not guarantee a reliable urban link. Delay includes sensing, communications, human response where relevant, processing and actuation.

03 · FICTIONAL LVIV CIVIC-BUILDING SURVEY

Seeing detail is not measuring depth

A calibrated stereo camera observes a facade. Moving farther away reduces disparity and increases depth uncertainty. Exposure time independently controls motion blur.

Camera inputs

u = fxX/Z + cx
Z = fB/d
σZ ≈ Z²σd/(fB)
GSD = Z/fpx
b ≈ fpxωtexp
f = 2100 px; image width = 3840 px; disparity error σd = 0.5 px. Facade is planar and normal to the optical axis. Blur uses radians/s and seconds.

This estimates measurement geometry, not autonomous navigation reliability. Glass, repeated windows, shadows, moving objects, low texture, calibration error and camera–IMU timing can dominate. Monocular vision alone cannot recover absolute metric scale without additional information.

04 · FICTIONAL POLTAVA TEMPORARY CIVIL COMMUNICATIONS SITE

A fibre link is also a mechanical system

A tethered camera or communications platform operates over a controlled site. The catenary panel isolates a cable span between equal-height supports; it is not a model of the complete ground-to-aircraft installation.

Equal-height cable-span model

w = μg; a = H/w
L = 2a sinh(X/2a)
sag = a[cosh(X/2a) − 1]
Tend = H cosh(X/2a)
Fdrag ≈ ½ρCD,tdtLV²
tfibre = ngL/c
Gravity-only exact catenary. Drag is shown separately, not applied to the curve. Cable diameter 4 mm; Cᴅ,t = 1.2; nɡ = 1.468. Uniform transverse flow assumed for the drag estimate.

Do not treat total distributed drag as the aircraft endpoint force. Endpoint load sharing needs the actual cable geometry and boundary conditions. Wind, unequal heights, payout, slack and snags require a more complete model.

Anchored hybrid tether

May combine strength members, fibre and conductors. Check cable weight, endpoint forces, working load, winch behavior, electrical loss and thermal limits.

Fibre-only payout

Provides a wired data path, not propulsion power. Deposited fibre, spool drag, bend damage and snagging are distinct from a suspended anchored catenary.

Use cable-assembly stiffness rather than glass-fibre stiffness alone. Modal frequencies assume uniform tension and ideal boundary conditions. Optical loss, bend limits, bandwidth and transceiver compatibility are separate checks.

Constant-speed delay and constant braking deceleration are simplifications. Enforce tilt, rotor, power and thermal constraints with anti-windup. Cable swept volume must also remain clear.
